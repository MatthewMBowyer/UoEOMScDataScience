Check-in

Event-anchored reminders to check in on each other, attached to occasions already on this page - NOT a fixed daily schedule. They land on the 1st and the 22nd of the month - the 22nd is the day of the month you started chatting - plus the three anniversaries above. That works out at roughly roughly weekly-to-fortnightly, rather than something that nags you every day.

DateWhat to do
22 Oct 2026Name one thing your partner did well this week, and tell them - not just think it.
